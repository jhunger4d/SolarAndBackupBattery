1. Public Opt-In Flow

  1. A user visits the public Solar and Backup Battery web form at https://solarandbackupbattery.com/quote (also accessible at https://solarandbackupbattery.com/), operated by GREEN INCENTIVES LLC.
  2. The user enters their mobile phone number in the field labeled “Mobile Phone Number (optional)”.
  3. To opt in to SMS, the user actively checks a single, optional unchecked SMS consent checkbox under the “Optional SMS Consent” heading. It is collected on its own and is not combined with any other consent or agreement.
  4. The checkbox’s consent language includes the Solar and Backup Battery brand name, message purpose, message frequency, message and data rates disclosure, STOP opt-out instructions, HELP instructions, support email, and consent-not-required language. The Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions appear only as reference links within the checkbox label; checking the box is consent solely to the SMS messages described and is not combined with agreement to any other document or service. Submitting the form is not conditioned on SMS consent or on agreeing to receive any calls or texts.
  5. If the user does not want text messages, they can leave the SMS consent checkbox unchecked and still submit the form, even if they provided a phone number for non-SMS specialist follow-up.

4. Consent Details

  • Each SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default.
  • The SMS consent is a single standalone checkbox; it requires its own affirmative action and is independent of the Privacy Policy and Terms documents and of submitting the form itself.
  • Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.
  • Message frequency varies based on the user’s inquiry and engagement.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Users can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
  • Support is available at jhunger4d@gmail.com or 209-565-0550.
  • We do not share, sell, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
